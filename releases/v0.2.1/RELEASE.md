# Realm 0.2.1

Your profiles, at a glance.

- See remaining five-hour and weekly Codex limits beneath every profile name in the sidebar.
- Compare usage and Free, Plus, or Pro plans directly in the Profiles view, with clock and calendar icons and the familiar green Running badge.
- Profile lists become more compact as your collection grows, with layouts that adapt to narrower windows.
- Usage refreshes across profiles using each account's own data and your chosen refresh interval. Unknown or expired limits show a dash.
- Profile Open and Stop now recover running apps after Realm restarts, avoid duplicate launches, wait for shutdown, and report immediate startup failures.

## Install

Download **Realm-macOS.dmg**, open it, and drag Realm to Applications. Quit your existing Realm copy before replacing it.

Apple silicon · macOS 14 or later · Version 0.2.1 (build 17).

The app is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized. macOS may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security on first launch. Claude limits remain available in Claude's own settings.

Screenshots use sample accounts. Percentages show remaining capacity. Windows remains at version 0.1.0.
